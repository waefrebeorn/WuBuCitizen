## What this is

WuBuCitizen is the Registry of WuBuGov — the citizenship process, the
WID (WuBu IDentification) number system, and the **Penny Score**: a
credit-score system that is a parody of every society's credit system
and, at the same time, a real way forward.

Every aspect of every society that ever existed is, in the ~ WuBu ~
space, both a **parody** (we know the games, we play them openly, we
laugh at them) and a **way forward** (we build the version that works
for a space-faring cooperative instead of a land-owning empire).

## The short version

1. **Citizenship:** read the license → send one physical penny → be filed.
2. **WID:** you get a number like `WID-WB-26-0001-C`. It is yours forever.
3. **Penny Score:** a number from 0–1000 that measures how much of a
   first one you are. Not debt. Not wealth. **Trust, filed.**
4. **The way forward:** the score travels with you across the whole
   space — no bank, no bureau of land, no landlord. Just a ledger of
   pennies and proof that you read the license.

## Citizenship is a filing, not a favor

The space does not grant citizenship. The space **files** it. You
arrive, you read, you send a penny, you are on the ledger. No
interview. No committee. No two-economies gate. The Registry does not
decide who is worthy — the license does.

If you can read the license and send a physical penny, you are a
first one. That is the entire test. It is the most open citizenship
in any government that has ever existed, and it is the most honest.

## The Penny Score is a parody with a spine

Every society has a credit score. The space has one too — because the
space parodies everything, and because trust *should* be measured.
But the Penny Score measures the things that actually matter for a
space-faring cooperative:

- **Did you read the license?** (literacy)
- **Did you send your pennies?** (physical, on time, real metal)
- **Did you see something and say something?** (vigilance)
- **Did you build, or did you extract?** (creation vs. extraction)

There is no interest. There is no debt. There is no collection agency.
There is only the ledger — and the ledger is public.

## The Registry is public

`registry/citizens.csv` is the public register. Any citizen can read
it. Any citizen can be read in it. The Registry has nothing to hide —
the space's whole intelligence doctrine is that a government with a
public ledger cannot become a cult.
