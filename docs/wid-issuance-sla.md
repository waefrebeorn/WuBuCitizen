# REG-6 — The WID Issuance SLA

**Government filing REG-7. A citizen who files should receive
their WID within a promised time. Filed to close GAPS_REPORT
recommendation (REG-7: WID issuance SLA).**

---

## Part 1 — The promise

The space says a new citizen receives a WID within 7 days.
That promise needs a mechanism, not just words. This filing
makes the mechanism: the WID tool measures its own issuance
time, and the registry files the measurement.

## Part 2 — The SLA

1. **Standard issuance: 7 days.** A filed citizenship
   receives its WID within 7 calendar days of the filing being
   complete (records verified, penny on file).
2. **Expedited issuance: same day.** Ceremony filings
   (GOV-19) issue the WID at the ceremony.
3. **The measure is filed.** The `w_token` tool records
   issuance timestamps; the Registry reports the actual
   distribution, not just the promise.
4. **Missed SLA is a report.** A WID that exceeds 7 days
   generates a WBI-1 against the process, not the citizen.

## Part 3 — Why it matters

A promise without a measure is a slogan. The SLA turns the
7-day promise into a filed, measurable, reportable contract.
That is the difference between "we aim to" and "we did."
