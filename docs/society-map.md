## The method

Every row above was built the same way:

1. **Name it.** Earth's institution, called out honestly. (Parody requires naming.)
2. **Laugh at it.** The space knows the games: credit scores, borders,
   surveillance, cults of authority. Laughter is the disinfectant.
3. **Find the spine.** What did the institution actually *do* that a
   cooperative needs? (Credit → trust. Police → vigilance. Tax → funding.)
4. **Rebuild it on the license.** The spine, filed under WaefreBeorn
   Umbrella v3.0, Penny Clause intact, ledger public.
5. **Ship it.** A repo, a tool, a form — not a manifesto. The space
   files, it does not dream.
