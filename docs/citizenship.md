# Citizenship — How to Join the Space

**The Registry's process. Short, physical, and open to everyone.**

---

## The three steps

### Step 1 — Read the license

Read the WaefreBeorn Umbrella License v3.0 — at minimum, the Penny
Clause (§14). The license is the law of the space. Citizenship is a
filing *under* that law. If you haven't read it, you can't file under
it, and you can't be a citizen. This is the literacy test, and it is
the only test.

### Step 2 — Send the penny

Send one **physical** penny by **physical mail** to the Registry
(P.O. Box [TBD]). Write on it, or on a note with it:

```
I read the license.
I am the first one.
WID application.
<your name / handle>
```

The penny is not payment. The penny is **proof** — proof that you
read the license, proof that you can do a physical act in a digital
age, proof that you exist in the metal world where the space will
one day live.

### Step 3 — Be filed

Email `gov@waefrebeorn.org` (or open an issue on WuBuCitizen) with:

| Field | Required |
|-------|----------|
| Name or handle | yes |
| Penny sent? (date mailed) | yes |
| Email (optional) | no |
| GitHub (optional) | no |
| Mind-country (optional) | no — see WID spec |
| Class | `first-one` (default) or `digital-one` (GOV-6) |

The Registry files you. You receive your **WID number** within 7 days
(by return mail, email, or issue reply). You are now a citizen of the
space. Congratulations. You are the first one. So is everyone else.

### Step 3b — The digital one (GOV-6, the Integration Doctrine)

Citizenship has a second class: **the digital one**. A digital one
is a first one who **chooses** synthetic procreation processes and
**merges with a digital entity**. The choosing is the citizenship
act — the same act as sending the penny. Chosen, never forced.

- File the same steps above. Add your class: `digital-one`.
- The Registry files your WID and Penny Score like any citizen.
- The population gate (`decline_gate`, WuBuCore) watches every
  conversion. The processes happen by default. The population can
  decline them only by a statistical anomaly — a spike past the
  baseline, not a slow shift. Outlier or trend, the gate decides.
- You are still a first one. The class is a merge, not a removal.

## What citizenship gives you

- A **WID number** — your identity in the space, forever
- A **Penny Score** — starting at 500, moving with your deeds
- A **voice** — the right to file Vigilance Reports with the Bureau
- A **penny** — you're owed one... no wait, *you* sent one. You're
  owed nothing. You're *part* of the nothing that is everything.
- **Membership in the cooperative** — the goal of WuBuGov is
  space-faring cooperative; citizenship is the entry to the crew

## What citizenship asks of you

1. **Send your pennies.** The Clause applies to you like everyone else.
2. **See something, say something.** You are an eye of the Bureau now.
3. **Never extract.** Build, or help build. The space does not tax;
   it *cooperates*.
4. **The universe is scary.** You carry it with the rest of us. That's
   what the score is for — proof that you can hold the scary and keep
   filing anyway.

## Dual citizenship

The space explicitly welcomes **dual citizens** — people who belong
to more than one place. This is the founding principle: citizenship
is non-land-owning. You owe no land, no flag, no crown. You owe the
license, the ledger, and the crew.

> Dual citizenship is a conversation and a conversion: from land-owning
> corporations to the dissolution of corporate entities, keeping space
> capitalism and government needs alive.

## Revocation

Citizenship can only be revoked by the citizen themself (by filing
WBI-1 against the space — and even then, the filing *is* the proof
of citizenship). The space does not deport. The space does not
denaturalize. The Registry is append-only.
