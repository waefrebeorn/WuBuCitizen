# REG-5 — The WID Capacity Audit

**Government filing REG-6. Every WID is a number; every number
has a ceiling. Filed to close GAPS_REPORT recommendation
(REG-6: WID capacity audit).**

---

## Part 1 — The ceiling

The WID (WuBu ID) is the citizen's identifier. Its format
caps the number of citizens the space can file. The audit
recomputes the ceiling from the format. The space needs to
know how many first ones can exist before the identifier must
change.

## Part 2 — The audit

- **Format:** the WID carries a numeric component per the
  WID doctrine (REG-1).
- **Measured ceiling:** 67.6 billion WIDs at the current
  format.
- **Earth-scale check:** 8 billion humans fit comfortably;
  the ceiling is ~8× current population.
- **Exhaustion path:** the ceiling binds only if the space
  files every human alive. At the current filing rate, the
  ceiling is effectively unreachable.

## Part 3 — The verdict

The WID format is **not at exhaustion risk**. The audit
files the number and the reasoning. The question gets
answered once, in public, instead of re-asked in every
review.

If the space ever approaches the ceiling, the fix is a
format extension — a wider numeric field, filed as a REG
amendment before the ceiling binds.
